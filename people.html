Principal Investigator

Dr. Laurianne Paravisini

Assistant Professor of Flavor Chemistry, University of Nebraska–Lincoln

Dr. Laurianne Paravisini is Assistant Professor of Flavor Chemistry at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln. Her expertise is understanding flavor through the integration of analytical chemistry, sensory science, and artificial intelligence. Dr. Paravisini has over 15 years of experience in flavor research across academia, startups, and global corporations, and aims to bring greater visibility to the field of flavor science while building the scientific foundation for flavor digitization.

She was trained in France in analytical chemistry then completed her M.S. in flavor and fragrance chemistry at the University of Montpellier and earned her Ph.D. in flavor chemistry at the Center for Taste and Feeding Behavior in Dijon. Prior to joining academia, she held scientific leadership roles at Osmo (Google spin-off), International Flavors and Fragrances (IFF), and Beyond Meat, where she led multidisciplinary teams spanning flavor chemistry, analytical chemistry and sensory science.
