University of Nebraska-Lincoln · Food Science & Technology

Understanding flavor, from molecule to perception.

The Flavor Intelligence Group studies the chemical and sensory underpinnings of flavor — how compounds form, release, and translate into what we taste and smell — to inform how food is made, evaluated, and improved.

About the lab

FIG is led by Dr. Laurianne [Last name], Assistant Professor in the Department of Food Science & Technology. The lab combines analytical flavor chemistry with sensory and consumer science to understand how flavor compounds are formed, delivered, and perceived — and to apply that understanding to real food and ingredient systems.

Before joining UNL, the lab's PI trained in flavor chemistry at the Université de Bourgogne and INRAE, worked at Ohio State's Flavor Research and Education Center, and held applied research roles in industry, including at Beyond Meat, IFF, and Osmo.

Research areas

Flavor chemistry

Identifying and characterizing the volatile and non-volatile compounds responsible for flavor in food and ingredient systems.

Analytical methods

Developing and applying chromatography, mass spectrometry, and related techniques to measure flavor compounds with precision.

Sensory science

Connecting chemical composition to human perception through sensory evaluation and consumer-focused research.
